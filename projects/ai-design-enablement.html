Overview

Our team faced a compounding challenge: a TypeScript migration and a full design system migration running simultaneously, with minimal designer bandwidth to support either. Engineering needed design guidance at scale, but there weren't enough hours in the day to consult on every component decision.

What I Did

I created a designrules.md — a structured document encoding the design rules for our product suite — and fed it into AI coding tools like Cursor. This gave developers the ability to generate design-compliant components without pulling a designer into every decision.

I also built a historical data roll-forward process that dramatically reduced the time and complexity of migrating existing components to the new design system, turning what had been a slow, error-prone handoff into a repeatable, fast workflow.

Impact

Recurring design consult requests — which ranged from 5 minutes to 2 hours each — were eliminated. Developers could make confident design decisions independently, and designers were freed to focus on harder, higher-value problems. One designer's expertise effectively scaled across the entire engineering team.

Role

Senior UX Designer. Led a team of 2 designers. Part of a multi-year design enablement effort aimed at tightening the loop between design and engineering.
