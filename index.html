My main goal: simplify complexity

I took a side door into product design. Early on, I started sitting in on design standups, taking on extra projects after hours, and enrolling in General Assembly to bridge the gap. I’ve been pulling up a chair ever since. Today, I’m a Staff Product Designer working at the intersection of complex B2B systems and AI tooling, balancing business outcomes with thoughtful craft.

My main goal is to turn complexity into simplicity. When things get messy, I resort to first principles: who we’re building for, the job to be done, and why it matters.

Outside of work, I'm a dad, a drummer, and a perpetual learner. Lately, that's meant AI coding tools, growing a garden for the first time, and trying to make shelves actually level.

There's always something to figure out. That part hasn't changed.
