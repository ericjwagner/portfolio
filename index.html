Playing on repeat

I didn't start as a designer. I pushed my way in. Showed up to the design team's standups uninvited, asked for work, enrolled in General Assembly. I've been pulling up chairs ever since.

Today I'm a senior product designer working at the intersection of complex B2B workflows and AI tooling, focused on outcomes as much as craft.

Every pixel needs a rationale. Design is a tool, not a statement. When things get murky I ask the dumb questions: Who are we designing for? What are the jobs to be done? Why would someone use this?

Outside work I'm a dad to an 18-month-old, a drummer, and a perpetual student. Lately that's meant AI coding tools, chess strategy, and why none of my shelves want to be level.

There's always something to figure out. That part hasn't changed.
