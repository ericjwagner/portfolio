I took a side door into product design. Early on, I started sitting in on design standups, taking on extra projects after hours, and enrolling in General Assembly to bridge the gap. I’ve been pulling up a chair ever since. Today, I’m a Staff Product Designer working at the intersection of complex B2B systems and AI tooling, balancing business outcomes with thoughtful craft.

For me, good design is intentional and grounded. When things get murky, I focus on first principles: who we’re building for, the job to be done, and why it matters.

Outside work, I'm a dad, a drummer, and a perpetual learner. Lately, that’s meant AI coding tools, growing a garden for the first time, and trying to make shelves actually level. There's always something to figure out. That part hasn't changed.
